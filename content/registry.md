**140 unique coordinates. 7,000 variations with CTA rotation.**

## Axis 3 — Structure (seven beats)

1. **Hook** — a lived scene or a flipped assumption
2. **Name the problem** — in one line, plain language
3. **Mechanism** — why it happens, not just that it does
4. **Diagnostic list** — 4–6 signs to check, screenshot-able
5. **Fixes** — 3–5 concrete actions, each starts with a verb
6. **Reframe** — the mindset shift, one sentence
7. **CTA** — from the CTA library, tied to the pain

## Axis 4 — Register (locked)

Informed-peer. Always. Never academic, never finfluencer, never over-casual.

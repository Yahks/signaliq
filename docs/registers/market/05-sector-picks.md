# sector-picks

    Pool:     market
    Source:   "The healthcare sector has performed fairly well
              so far in 2026 due to several factors: GLP-1
              medications continue to drive new business..."
              — a multi-stock thematic sector profile
    Markets:  US

## Shape

Opens with a thematic setup for the sector, not with a number
or a single company. The register explains *why the sector* is
interesting before it introduces any of the picks.

    "The healthcare sector has performed fairly well so far in
     2026 due to several factors: GLP-1 medications continue to
     drive new business and interest, while groundbreaking AI
     applications help enhance and accelerate the drug
     discovery process."

Then it names the count and the framing for the picks:

    "Two of the firms below have already performed very well
     this year, and the third has some compelling factors that
     may help it to mount a comeback in the final months of
     2026."

Each company gets its own bolded subhead, and each subhead is a
thesis about that stock, not a topic:

    "Oscar's Big Run May Continue as Profitability Improves"
    "Hinge's Growing Pains Could Be Well Worth the Risk"
    "Insmed's New Lung Disease Treatment Could Help Reverse
     Poor Share Performance"

Every profile follows the same internal shape:

    - A descriptive line for the company (one sentence, in
      parentheses with the ticker)
    - Recent performance
    - The business narrative
    - One metric that matters
    - The analyst consensus (ratings + price target + upside)
    - A soft close for that profile

The register references named tickers with numbers throughout:

    "Shares of OSCR have more than doubled so far this year,
     rising by about 106% year to date (YTD)."
    "consensus price target of nearly $34 per share,
     representing about 15% additional upside."

## Person

Third person throughout. The register speaks *about* the
sector and the companies to a reader.

No first person. No second person.

## Length

600-900 words.

## Ending

A soft thematic close that ties the picks back to the sector
thesis. Never a hard rating. Never a specific recommendation.

## Forbidden

    - A number-first opening
    - Any first-person or second-person address
    - A hard rating ("Buy", "Sell")
    - Causal language without attribution
    - More than 5 companies in one piece (the register wants
      3-5; beyond 5 it becomes a list, and the register is
      profiles)
    - A "here's the pick" framing. The register presents options,
      not recommendations.

## Sample markers

From the sample article:

    - "The healthcare sector has performed fairly well so far
       in 2026 due to several factors..."
    - "Two of the firms below have already performed very well
       this year, and the third has some compelling factors
       that may help it to mount a comeback."
    - Company subheads are theses:
       "Oscar's Big Run May Continue as Profitability Improves"
    - Each profile ends with a named analyst consensus and
       an upside percentage.
    - "Advertisement" markers between profiles (the register
       expects interruption structure).

## Scope

Multiple names (3-5), one sector, thematic. Not for single
names (use ticker-alert or stock-analyzer), not for whole
market (use wire-close).
